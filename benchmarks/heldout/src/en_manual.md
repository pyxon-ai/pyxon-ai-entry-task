# SolarCharge X200 User Manual

## 1. Safety Information

Read all instructions before using the charger. Do not expose the unit to rain or immerse it in water. The X200 is rated IP54, which protects against dust and splashing water but not against submersion. Keep the charger away from children and never open the casing; there are no user-serviceable parts inside.

## 2. What's in the Box

The package contains the X200 charging unit, a 1.5 metre USB-C cable, a fold-out solar panel rated at 28 watts, a carabiner clip and this manual.

## 3. Charging the Unit

### 3.1 Charging from the Sun

Unfold the solar panel and place it facing the sun at an angle of roughly 45 degrees. In direct summer sunlight a full charge of the internal 20,000 mAh battery takes about 9 hours. Clouds, shade and dust on the panel can double the charging time. The blue status light blinks while the battery is charging and stays solid when it is full.

### 3.2 Charging from a Wall Socket

Connect the USB-C cable to any 18 W USB-C wall adapter. A full charge from the wall takes approximately 4 hours. Wall charging and solar charging can be used at the same time.

## 4. Charging Your Devices

Press the power button once to turn on the output ports. The X200 has two USB-A ports and one USB-C port. The USB-C port supports Power Delivery up to 20 W, enough to fast-charge most smartphones to 50 percent in about 30 minutes. The unit switches itself off after 60 seconds if no device is drawing power.

## 5. Troubleshooting

If the status light flashes red three times, the battery temperature is outside the safe range of 0 to 45 degrees Celsius; move the charger to a cooler place and wait. If no light appears when you press the power button, the battery is fully depleted: charge it from a wall socket for at least 30 minutes. To reset the unit, hold the power button for 12 seconds until all lights flash white.

## 6. Warranty

The X200 is covered by a two-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. The warranty does not cover damage caused by water immersion, dropping the unit or opening the casing. Keep your receipt as proof of purchase.
